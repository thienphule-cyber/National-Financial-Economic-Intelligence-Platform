# References

Sources are grouped by purpose. Numbers (R1, R2, ...) are the identifiers used in `report.md`.

> Check each link and citation detail before using them in formal academic work. Web pages move, and bibliographic details should be verified against the original publication.

**R1.** World Bank. *World Bank Open Data.* https://data.worldbank.org

**R2.** World Bank. *Indicators API documentation.* https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api-documentation

## Economic theory

**R7.** Harrod, R. F. (1939). An essay in dynamic theory. *The Economic Journal*, 49(193), 14-33.

**R8.** Domar, E. D. (1946). Capital expansion, rate of growth, and employment. *Econometrica*, 14(2), 137-147.

**R10.** Kuznets, S. (1957). Quantitative aspects of the economic growth of nations: II. Industrial distribution of national product and labor force. *Economic Development and Cultural Change*, 5(4, Supplement), 1-111.

**R14.** Hyndman, R. J., & Athanasopoulos, G. (2021). *Forecasting: Principles and Practice* (3rd ed.). OTexts. https://otexts.com/fpp3/
